Honest advice

When Joon is the right choice

Your child loves pets

Looking after a virtual pet is a strong daily pull for many six to twelve year olds.

You want an ADHD-focused design

Joon is designed with ADHD and focus in mind, and says so throughout.

Your child has their own device

Joon’s kids’ app works well when a child has a tablet or phone to use on their own.

Honest advice

When to look elsewhere

Your children are under six

Picture-led quests and a press-and-hold to finish suit younger children. Score Your Chores starts from about three.

Everything runs on one family phone

If your children don’t have devices, an app built for one shared phone is simpler. See shared phone chore apps.

You want the whole family on one team

Score Your Chores adds a weekly Family Cup the whole family plays for, grown-ups included.

You want two children fully covered for free

Score Your Chores is free for two children with every core feature and no ads. Joon also has a free version, so compare what each free plan includes.

Questions parents ask

Is there a free alternative to Joon?

Yes. Score Your Chores is free for two children with every core feature and no ads. FamilyQuest is a free browser app, and NatWest Rooster Money has a free star chart and pocket money tracker.

Is Joon available in the UK?

Yes. Joon is available on the UK App Store and Google Play. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe.

What is a good Joon alternative for younger children?

For children from about three to eight, Score Your Chores gives each child a Buddy that grows with every quest, and Neat Kid is a simple star chart.

Which Joon alternative works without a kids’ app?

Score Your Chores runs on one shared family phone with no child accounts, so there is no separate kids’ app to install.
