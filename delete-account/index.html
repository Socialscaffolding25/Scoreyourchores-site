Account deletion

Request account deletion.

Signed-in parents can request deletion of their ScoreYourChores cloud account and household data from inside the app or by email.

In the app

Open ScoreYourChores, go to Parent Settings, and use the account deletion request option. This creates a support request linked to your signed-in parent account.

By email

Email info@labiireatum.co.uk from the parent account email address and include "Account deletion" in the subject line.

What is deleted

Cloud account, household data, and stored child profile photos associated with the parent account will be deleted after verification. Deleting the app also removes local device data from that device.

Subscriptions

Deleting your ScoreYourChores account does not cancel an Apple App Store subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your App Store account settings.

Manual family rewards

ScoreYourChores does not process reward payments, transfers, deposits, withdrawals, or cash-outs. Manual family rewards are handled outside the app.
