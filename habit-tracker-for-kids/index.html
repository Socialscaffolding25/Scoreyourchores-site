Free printable habit tracker for kids

A habit tracker is a simple grid: one row for each habit and a box for each day. Children tick the box when they do it, and by Sunday they can see the week they had. Choose three to five habits, add your child’s name and print it.

Tips

Making habits stick

Start small

Three to five habits that stick beat ten that are forgotten by February. Add one more once the first few feel easy.

Make them small

“Read for 20 minutes” works better than “read more”. Children need to know exactly when they can tick the box.

Track, don’t punish

A blank box is information, not a failure. Leave it, carry on tomorrow and never take ticks away.

Review every Sunday

Look at the week together. Keep the habits that worked, swap one that did not, and print a fresh tracker.

Questions parents ask

What is a habit tracker for kids?

A grid with a row for each habit and a box for each day of the week. Children tick the box on the days they do the habit, so they can see their progress.

How many habits should a child track?

Start with three to five. A short list they can manage every day builds more confidence than a long one.

What age is a habit tracker good for?

From about seven, when children can read the list and tick it themselves. Younger children do better with a picture routine chart.

What if they miss a day?

Leave the box blank and carry on the next day. The point is the overall pattern across the week, not a perfect row.
