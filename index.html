How it works

A chore chart and reward chart in one.

1. Add your kids

First names and ages. Score Your Chores suggests age-matched chores for each child, from making the bed to emptying the dishwasher.

2. Kids wake up their Buddy

Six Buddies to choose from. Kids tap theirs awake, then grow it with every quest and unlock outfits they earn, never buy.

3. Quests, then confetti

Kids see just today's quests. Press and hold to finish. You approve with a quick "Brilliant!" and the points land.

4. Rewards you choose

Screen time, staying up later, picking Friday's dinner or optional pocket money. Kids ask, you say yes. You stay in charge.

The Family Cup

Everyone's points count towards a weekly team goal, like pizza night. Friendly competition, or pure teamwork: your choice.

Sunday Scores

A weekly reveal the whole family gathers round for, with awards for top scorer, longest streak and most improved. Share the scorecard with the grandparents.

Questions parents ask

What chores should kids start with?

Start small: make the bed, put toys away, feed the pet or lay the table. Score Your Chores suggests age-matched chores for each child, and three or four each is the sweet spot for the first week.

How do points, rewards and pocket money work?

Every finished quest earns points. You choose the rewards, like screen time or picking Friday's dinner, and kids ask for them when they have enough points. Pocket money is optional and you hand it over yourself: no money moves through the app.

Do children need their own phone or account?

No. Score Your Chores works on one shared family iPhone. Each child taps their own card on the Who's up screen, and a grown-up PIN keeps approvals and settings for parents.

Is Score Your Chores free?

Yes. The free plan includes two children and every core feature: chores, approvals, rewards, all six Buddies, the Family Cup and Sunday Scores. Optional Plus adds more children, photo proof, bonus Buddy outfits and past Sunday Scores, with a free trial where offered.
