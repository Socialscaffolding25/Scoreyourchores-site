Built for families

Simple enough for daily use.

The app is designed for parent-supervised households. Children do not need email addresses, the app does not process reward payments, and parents stay in control of setup, scoring, and rewards.

Chores and scores

Create recurring chores, review submissions, and keep a simple family leaderboard that makes progress visible.

Manual rewards

Reward values are virtual. Parents handle real-world rewards outside the app and can record them as given afterwards.

Parent account backup

Parents can create an account to back up household setup and sync the family scoreboard when online.

Privacy-light by design

No ads, analytics trackers, cross-app tracking, contacts, camera, location, or push notifications are used in the current release.
