How it works

Three steps, every day

  1. 1 Kids follow their quests

    Each child taps their own name and sees just today's jobs, from making the bed to feeding the pet. Press and hold to finish.

  2. 2 You check and approve

    Finished quests wait for a grown-up. Approve with a quick "Brilliant!" or a bonus, or send one back for another go.

  3. 3 They save up for rewards you choose

    Points go towards rewards you set, like screen time or picking Friday's dinner. Kids ask for them; you decide.

Questions parents ask

Do children need their own phone or account?

No. Score Your Chores works on one shared family iPhone. Each child taps their own card on the Who's up screen, and a grown-up PIN keeps approvals, rewards and settings for parents.

Is Score Your Chores free?

Yes. The free plan includes two children and every core feature: chores, approvals, rewards, all six Buddies, the Family Cup and Sunday Scores. Optional Plus adds more children, photo proof, bonus Buddy outfits and every past Sunday Scores. In the UK it costs £3.99 a month or £29.99 a year, and the yearly plan starts with a one-week free trial.

How do points, rewards and pocket money work?

Every finished quest earns points. You choose the rewards, like screen time or picking Friday's dinner, and kids ask for them when they have enough points. Pocket money is optional and you hand it over yourself: no money moves through the app.

What chores should kids start with?

Start small: make the bed, put toys away, feed the pet or lay the table. Score Your Chores suggests age-matched chores for each child, and three or four each is plenty for the first week.
