Worked example

What a family with three children gets for free

Take a family with three children aged 4, 7 and 10 and one phone between them.

  • Score Your Chores: two of the children are covered free with every core feature. Adding the third needs Plus, at £3.99 a month or £29.99 a year.
  • PointUp: the free plan allows up to three family members and five active quests, so check whether a parent counts towards the three; Pro removes the limits.
  • FamilyQuest: free for all three, used in a web browser rather than an app.
  • S’moresUp: the free Standard plan has no limit on children or chores.
  • Paper: a printable chart per child costs nothing but the printing.

If your youngest is a pre-reader, check that the app shows pictures, not just words, before you commit.

Questions parents ask

Is there a completely free chore app for kids?

Yes. Score Your Chores is free for two children with every core feature and no ads, and FamilyQuest says it has no paid tier. S’moresUp has a free Standard plan, and NatWest Rooster Money’s tracker and star chart are free too.

What is the best free chore app in the UK?

For children aged about 3 to 12 on one shared family phone, Score Your Chores: its free plan covers two children with every core feature and no ads. If you would rather use a browser than an app, FamilyQuest is free.

Do free chore apps have adverts?

Some do. Score Your Chores, FamilyQuest and Family Tools say they have no adverts. Check any app’s store listing before you install it.

Is a printable chore chart better than an app?

Both work. Paper suits a single goal or younger children; an app adds parent approval, rewards and a running total without redrawing the chart each week.
