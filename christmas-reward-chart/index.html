Free printable Christmas reward chart

A festive sticker chart for December. Add your child’s name, their own Buddy and the Christmas treat they are working towards, choose how many stickers it takes, then print it on A4 and pop it on the fridge.

Tips

A calmer December

One goal for the month

Getting dressed first time, kind words, staying in bed. One clear goal is easier to stick to in a busy month than a long list.

Count down together

Twenty stickers fits from 1 December to the school holidays. Put the chart next to the advent calendar so both get done each morning.

Festive rewards, not presents

Choosing the Christmas film or putting the star on the tree feels special and keeps presents out of it.

Never take stickers away

If today was tricky there is simply no sticker. Stickers they have earned stay earned, even in the week before Christmas.

Questions parents ask

How does a Christmas reward chart work?

Pick one thing to work on in December, like getting dressed first time or kind words with a brother or sister. Each time it happens your child adds a sticker, and when the chart is full they get the festive reward you agreed at the start.

Can I use it as a Christmas behaviour chart?

Yes. Write the behaviour you want to see as the goal, in positive words: “Kind hands” rather than “No hitting”. Stickers are only ever added, never taken away, so a tough day simply means no sticker that day.

How many stickers should a Christmas reward chart have?

Twenty stickers fits nicely between 1 December and the start of the school holidays. For children under five, choose 10 so the first reward comes within a week.

What festive rewards can kids earn?

Choosing the Christmas film, hot chocolate by the tree, putting the star on top, an extra bedtime story, baking mince pies together or a trip to see the Christmas lights. Keep their presents out of it.

Should I use the naughty list?

We would keep it gentle. Children respond better to earning a sticker for good choices than to warnings about presents, and it keeps December fun for everyone.
