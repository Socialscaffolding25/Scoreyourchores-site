Questions parents ask

What chores can a 5 year old do?

At five, most children can make their bed (roughly), lay and clear the table, put toys away, feed a pet, sort socks, water plants and put clean clothes in drawers. Show them once, then let them do it their way.

What chores can a 10 year old do?

By ten, children can hoover, load and empty the dishwasher, take the bins out, clean the bath, hang out washing, make a simple meal with help and walk the dog with someone.

How many chores should a child have?

Two to four small daily chores suits most primary school children, plus one or two bigger weekly jobs from around age nine. Fewer chores done every day beat a long list that gets ignored.

How do I get my child to do chores without nagging?

Put the chores somewhere they can see them, tick them off straight away, agree the reward in advance and review together at the end of the week. A chart or an app can take on some of the reminding.
