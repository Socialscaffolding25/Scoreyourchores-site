Support

Help for ScoreYourChores families.

ScoreYourChores is provided by Labii Reatum LTD. Contact info@labiireatum.co.uk for help with setup, sync, subscriptions, account deletion, or family reward records.

What to include

Include your device model, iOS version, the screen you were using, what happened, and what you expected to happen. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Family setup

Use Settings in the app to edit the household name, child names, optional profile photos, parent account state, and sync status.

Manual rewards

ScoreYourChores does not move money. If a child requests a family reward, handle that reward outside the app and record it as given afterwards.

Before deleting the app

If you are signed in, confirm Settings shows a synced state before deleting the app. If you are using local setup only, deleting the app removes the local scoreboard.

Account deletion

To request cloud account and household data deletion, use the in-app Settings screen or visit Delete account.

Subscriptions

Optional Plus subscriptions are handled through Apple In-App Purchase on iPhone and Google Play Billing on Android. To manage or cancel, use your Apple Account subscriptions settings on iPhone, or Google Play, Payments and subscriptions on Android.
