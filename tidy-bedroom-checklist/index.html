Free printable tidy bedroom checklist

“Tidy your room” is too big an instruction for most children. Break it into small steps they can tick off one at a time, and “done” finally means the same thing to both of you.

Tips

Making it stick

One step at a time

A messy room can feel overwhelming. Work down the checklist in order and only look at the next step.

Show what done looks like

Tidy the room together once and take a photo. Stick it next to the checklist so “done” means the same thing to both of you.

Beat the timer

Set a timer for ten minutes and see how far down the list they get. A race against the clock beats a lecture.

A home for everything

Tidying is much easier when every toy has a box and every box has a picture label.

Questions parents ask

How do I get my child to tidy their room?

Break the job into small steps, show them once what done looks like, and let them tick off each step. A short daily tidy is easier than a big weekly one.

At what age can a child tidy their own room?

From around four, most children can put toys in boxes and clothes in the basket with a reminder. By seven or eight, many can work through a checklist on their own.
