OurHome alternatives for UK families (2026)

OurHome was a popular free chore and family organiser app. It has been discontinued and its mobile apps are no longer available. Here are the apps that do the same jobs today. We make Score Your Chores, so we have said plainly where another app fits better.

What happened

What happened to OurHome?

OurHome let families share chores, points and rewards, with a family calendar and shopping list alongside. It has since been discontinued: its mobile apps are no longer available, according to AlternativeTo. Most families replace it with a chore app for the children and, if they need one, a separate family calendar.

Moving across

Starting again after OurHome

Write down the balances

Before you switch, note each child’s chores and saved points so nobody feels they lost out.

Honour what was saved

Agree with the children how their old points carry over, for example as a starting bonus or one reward straight away.

Start smaller than before

Three or four chores each for the first week. Add more once the new routine sticks.

Let them choose

Children take to a new app faster when they help set it up and pick their own character.

Questions parents ask

Has OurHome shut down?

Yes. The original OurHome app has been discontinued and its mobile apps are no longer available to download.

What is the best free alternative to OurHome?

For children’s chores and rewards, Score Your Chores is free for two children with every core feature and no ads, and FamilyQuest is a free browser app. For a shared calendar and shopping list, Cozi and Family Tools both have free versions.

Is there an OurHome alternative that works on one family phone?

Yes. Score Your Chores is built for one shared family phone: each child taps their own card and there are no child accounts.

Which OurHome alternative has a calendar?

Family Tools, S’moresUp and Cozi all include a family calendar. Score Your Chores focuses on chores and rewards for children.
