Free printable after-school routine chart

The hour after school sets up the whole evening. Put the steps on a chart, agree the order once, and the “have you done your reading?” conversation mostly disappears.

Tips

Making it stick

Snack first

Most children come home hungry and tired. A snack and ten minutes off before anything else makes the rest easier.

Homework before screens

Agree that screens come after the chart is finished, so it never becomes a daily debate.

Pack tonight, not tomorrow

Bag and uniform ready the evening before takes two steps out of the morning rush.

Keep club nights simple

On busy days, cut the chart down to the three steps that really matter.

Questions parents ask

What should an after-school routine include?

Unpack (shoes, coat, lunchbox), a snack, homework or reading, getting the bag and uniform ready for tomorrow, and some time to play.

When should children do homework after school?

Many children do better after a snack and a short break, but before they get too tired or start on screens. Pick a time and keep it the same each day.
