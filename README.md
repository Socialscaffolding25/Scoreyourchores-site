# Score Your Chores website

Source for **[scoreyourchores.com](https://scoreyourchores.com/)**, the website for Score Your Chores: a family chore chart and reward chart app for children aged about 3 to 12, on iPhone and Android. Each child grows their own cartoon Buddy by finishing chores, parents approve in two taps and choose the rewards, and it all runs on one shared family phone with no child accounts and no ads. Made in the UK by Labii Reatum LTD.

- Download: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/scoreyourchores/id6795851917) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scoreyourchores.app)
