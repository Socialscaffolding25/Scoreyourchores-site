Kindness notes for your elf

24 notes for your elf to leave, one each December morning, signed with your elf’s name. The first and last greet your child by name. Some tell your child something kind your elf spotted, some give them a gentle mission for the day. Free to print, and they only ever notice the good.

How it works

Five minutes before 1 December

1. Add the names

Your child’s name and your elf’s name. Pick a colour.

2. Print and cut

Two A4 pages, 12 notes each. Cut along the dotted lines.

3. One note a night

Leave the next note with your elf. On “I spotted you” notes, write one real kind thing from that day.

4. Read it together

Read the note at breakfast. Being seen for the good is what makes it work.

Questions parents ask

Can I use these with an elf on the shelf?

Yes. Type your elf’s name and every note is signed by them. They work just as well for a Kindness Buddy, a reindeer or any December visitor. Leave the name blank and they are signed “your elf”.

What is on the notes?

24 notes, one for each morning from 1 to 24 December. Some say “I spotted you” with space to write one kind thing you saw that day. Others give a gentle mission, like making someone smile or helping lay the table. The first says hello and the last says Happy Christmas.

Why only kind things?

Children try hardest when they feel noticed for what they get right. Notes that catch your child being kind turn December into a game of being good to each other, without the worry of a naughty list.

What do I need?

A printer and scissors. The notes print on two A4 pages, 12 to a page. Cut along the dotted lines and leave one out each night.

We don’t have an elf. Can we still use them?

Yes. The free Kindness Buddy kit has a stand-up paper friend, its letter to your child and the same 24 notes, so you don’t need anything else.
