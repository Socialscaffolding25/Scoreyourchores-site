Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 July 2026. Last updated: 3 October 2026. ScoreYourChores is operated by Labii Reatum LTD and is a family chore scoreboard for parents and children. This policy covers the iPhone and Android apps and the scoreyourchores.com website.

Account and household data

Parents can create an account to back up household setup, child profiles, chores, scores, approvals, reward requests, and settings. Children do not need email addresses or online accounts.

Local device data

The app keeps local device state so the family scoreboard remains usable during normal app use. Deleting the app removes local device data from that device.

Cloud backup and sync

Signed-in parent households can sync household data to our backend provider so the scoreboard can be backed up and restored. Access is protected by parent account authentication.

No tracking

The app does not use advertising identifiers, analytics trackers, third-party ads, cross-app tracking, contact access, location access, or a push notification service. Optional daily and trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Camera permission is requested only when a child takes an optional completed-chore photo.

Optional Plus subscription

ScoreYourChores offers an optional Plus subscription through Apple In-App Purchase on iPhone and Google Play Billing on Android. Plus unlocks extras such as more child profiles, photo proof, bonus Buddy outfits and past Sunday Scores. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see card details.

No reward payments

Reward pot values are virtual family-managed values. Parents may record that a manual reward was given outside the app, but ScoreYourChores does not process reward payments, transfers, deposits, withdrawals, or cash-outs.

Children's privacy

Child profiles are managed by parents and are used only for the household scoreboard, its share cards and, when a grown-up chooses, printable charts (see below). Families should not enter sensitive personal information into chore titles, notes, or child profile names.

Optional profile photos

A parent may choose an optional child profile photo. Selected photos are cropped and uploaded to private storage for the signed-in family. They are shown using time-limited links and are not used for tracking, advertising, or identity recognition. Parents can remove them in Family.

Optional chore photos

With Plus, a parent can ask for a photo of a finished chore. Without a family account the photo stays on the device and is deleted when a grown-up approves the quest or sends it back. In a signed-in family account, photos use private storage and time-limited links and the app requests removal after review; if cleanup fails, the private file may remain until account deletion. Photos should show the job, not faces.

Share cards

Team cards and Sunday Scores cards are created on your device and are only shared if you choose to. They show first names, Buddies and points.

Printable charts from the app

A grown-up can open a child's chart on our website to print it. To fill it in, the app puts the child's first name, Buddy, colour, age group and up to eight quest titles in the web address. That address reaches our website host, GitHub Pages, when the page loads. The page then removes those details from the address bar, so they are not left in a copied link or bookmark. This cannot undo that first request, or remove what your browser may already have recorded.

Our website

scoreyourchores.com does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers. Charts made on the website are drawn in your browser and are not sent to us. Most pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when they open. App Store links on the website carry a short label naming the page or channel, such as web-reward-chart, so App Store Connect can show us how many first-time downloads came from each one. Apple reports these only as totals and withholds small numbers; a label never identifies a person or family.

Deleting data

To delete cloud account, household data, and stored profile or chore photos, use Settings in the app to request account deletion, or contact info@labiireatum.co.uk.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@labiireatum.co.uk. The same address handles app support and account-deletion questions.
