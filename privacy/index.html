Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 July 2026. ScoreYourChores is operated by Labii Reatum LTD and is a family chore scoreboard for parents and children. This policy describes the current iOS release.

Account and household data

Parents can create an account to back up household setup, child profiles, chores, scores, approvals, reward requests, and settings. Children do not need email addresses or online accounts.

Local device data

The app keeps local device state so the family scoreboard remains usable during normal app use. Deleting the app removes local device data from that device.

Cloud backup and sync

Signed-in parent households can sync household data to our backend provider so the scoreboard can be backed up and restored. Access is protected by parent account authentication.

No tracking

The current release does not use advertising identifiers, analytics trackers, third-party ads, cross-app tracking, contact access, location access, camera access, or push notifications. The system photo picker opens only when a parent chooses a profile photo.

Optional Plus subscription

ScoreYourChores may offer an optional Plus subscription through Apple In-App Purchase. Plus unlocks larger family routines, such as more child profiles and active chores. App subscription purchases are handled by Apple.

No reward payments

Reward pot values are virtual family-managed values. Parents may record that a manual reward was given outside the app, but ScoreYourChores does not process reward payments, transfers, deposits, withdrawals, or cash-outs.

Children's privacy

Child profiles are managed by parents and are used only for the household scoreboard. Families should not enter sensitive personal information into chore titles, notes, or child profile names.

Optional profile photos

A parent may choose an optional child profile photo. Selected photos are cropped and uploaded to private storage for the signed-in family. They are shown using time-limited links and are not used for tracking, advertising, or identity recognition. Parents can remove them in Settings.

Deleting data

To delete cloud account, household data, and stored profile photos, use Settings in the app to request account deletion, or contact info@labiireatum.co.uk.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@labiireatum.co.uk. The same address handles app support and account-deletion questions.
