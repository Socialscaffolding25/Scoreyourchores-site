Free printable screen time checklist

Screens after the important things, not instead of them. Agree the list once, stick it by the TV or the tablet charger, and let the chart do the reminding.

Tips

Making it stick

Agree it together

Let your child help choose the list. They are far more likely to follow rules they helped write.

Keep it short

Four to six ticks is enough. A list that takes all evening makes screens feel impossible to reach.

Same rule every day

The checklist works because it is predictable. Change it at the weekend if you need to, not in the middle of an argument.

Put it where the screens live

Next to the TV, the games console or where the tablet charges.

Questions parents ask

What should kids do before screen time?

Most families pick homework, reading, a tidy bedroom, one job to help the family and some time outside or active play.

How much screen time should children have?

There is no single right number for every family. Agree a daily amount that suits your child’s age and your routine, and use the checklist so the must-dos happen first.
