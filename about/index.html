About

About Score Your Chores

Score Your Chores is a chore chart and reward chart app for families, made in the UK. Here are the key facts in one place, for parents, press and anyone comparing chore apps.

Key facts

Score Your Chores at a glance

What it isA family chore chart and reward chart app. Children finish quests (chores) to grow their own cartoon Buddy and earn points for rewards their parents choose.
Who it is forFamilies with children aged about 3 to 12, with a teen mode for older children. Grown-ups can join the team too.
Where it runsiPhone and Android, on one shared family phone. Each child taps their own card; there are no child accounts.
PriceFree for two children with every core feature. Optional Plus adds more children, photo proof, bonus Buddy outfits and past Sunday Scores: £3.99 a month or £29.99 a year in the UK, and the yearly plan starts with a one-week free trial.
Privacy and safetyNo adverts and no tracking. Children never see prices or buy buttons, and a grown-up PIN protects approvals, rewards and settings. Pocket money is optional and handed over by parents: no money moves through the app.
Key featuresPicture quests matched to each child’s age, parent approval in two taps, rewards you choose, six Buddies to grow, a weekly Family Cup team goal and a Sunday Scores reveal with awards.
Free printablesPersonalised printable chore charts, reward charts, routine charts and seasonal charts on this website, with no sign-up.
Made byLabii Reatum LTD, a UK company. Score Your Chores was first released in 2026.
